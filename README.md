### What I build

**Conversational agents on the Anthropic Claude API with tool use** — over text and over real-time voice. They read live data through tools and take real, bounded actions. The tool surface is deliberately narrow, so the agent cannot invent a price or an item that does not exist: the safety property lives in the tool boundary, not in the prompt, which means it holds even when the model surprises me.

**Mobile apps in Flutter** for iOS and Android — published and maintained on the App Store and Google Play through continuous releases.

**Backends in Node.js and TypeScript** — REST APIs, WebSockets, role-based access control.

**Payment layers, end to end** — card and mobile payments, transfer validation, automated payouts to couriers and merchants, commission engines, in-app wallet, electronic fiscal invoicing.

**Identity verification** — face matching and on-device document OCR.

**Python automation** — a catalogue matcher that classifies products by image, brand and barcode: 1,172 products at roughly 99% accuracy, replacing work that was being done by hand.

**Web** — React sites and applications I design, build and maintain.

### How I work

I build **AI-native**. I own the architecture and direct the system into existence with Claude Code rather than typing every line. That is the only reason one engineer can ship and maintain a platform this wide: an ordering, delivery and ride-hailing app live on iOS and Android, with merchants, couriers, bank payments and electronic invoicing — where I am the sole engineer.
